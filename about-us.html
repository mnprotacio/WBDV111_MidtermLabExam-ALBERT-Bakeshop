About Us

Our Story

It started the way most great things do — with passion, a little flour, and a whole lot of heart.

In 2022, Albert's Bakeshop opened its doors along Sgt. P. Bernardo, Brgy. Caniogan, Pasig City, with nothing more than a dream to create cakes that people would remember long after the last slice was gone.

What began as a humble neighborhood bakeshop quickly became a staple in the community. Families came for the cakes, but kept coming back for the feeling — the warmth of knowing that every order was made with intention, every detail handled with care.

We believe that a cake is never just a cake. It is a centerpiece at your child's first birthday, the sweetest part of your wedding day, the surprise that makes someone feel truly seen. That is what drives us — not just to bake, but to be part of the moments that matter most to you.

Three years in, and our love for the craft has only grown stronger. We remain proudly rooted in Pasig, serving every occasion with the same dedication we had on day one.

Albert's Bakeshop. Baked with love, made for you.
